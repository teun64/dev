2G Migratie mails: draft for marketing

Three Growth mails for the 2G Migratie campaign: mail 1 on the day a batch is released, mail 2 after 5 days and mail 3 after 12 days, the reminders only to customers who haven't chosen yet. Built on marketing's own prod mail 2g-4gvervangen (same header, logo, wording and button style). Texts are a first proposal; marketing owns the final wording.

Where the drafts are

  • dev Growth, folder Moving Intelligence/Campaigns/Year 2026/M20261109 2G Migratie: “M20261109 2G Migratie - mail 1/2/3”.
  • Not in prod or acc. A prod copy is ready (folder Emails/M20261109 2G Migratie, prod image references) and goes in only on an explicit go.

The choice buttons

  • Each button opens the customer's own response page, where they confirm their choice with one click.
  • Mail 1 and 2: Bel mij terug, Stuur mij een offerte, Nu niet, later (the page asks for Q1–Q4 2027).
  • No dealer choice in the mails: "Processed by dealer" is recorded by sales on the phone script.
  • Mail 3 adds Geen interesse.

Preference center

  • The footer links to the customer's own Customer Hub preference page (decision 2026-10-09), not Growth's unsubscribe link.
  • Check before sending: if Growth refuses a promotional send without its own opt-out link, add it next to ours.
